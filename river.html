River


    Davidson's Mains Park is a community hub for local residents, offering a safe and accessible outdoor space for families, dog walkers, and those seeking a quiet retreat from the urban environment. The park is maintained by the City of Edinburgh Council and is free to access year-round.


    Key Features & Facilities


  • Play Area: climbing frames, slides, swings, and play structures suited for younger children.

  • Woodland: Offers open grass fields for recreation alongside mature tree belts ideal for dog walking and short strolls.

  • Links: Directly connects to local walking and cycle paths leading toward Lauriston Castle, the grounds of Cramond, and the nearby old railway paths (Silverknowes Path network).

  • Location & Access: Situated off Quality Street and East Barnton Avenue, within walking distance of the Davidson's Mains village main street shops and Lothian bus routes (lines 21, 41, and 47).
